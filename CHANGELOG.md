## 1.0.0 (2026-10-31)

First release.

- **Share target:** in Instagram, share a reel to **Reel Saver** and the video is saved to **Movies/Reel Saver** in your gallery, with a notification when it's done.
- **Paste a link:** open the app and paste a reel link to download it the same way.
- No Instagram login is used or asked for, so reels from private accounts can't be saved; the app tells you plainly when a reel can't be fetched.
